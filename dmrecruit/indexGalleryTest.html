About Us

The Parkland Digital Media program is focused on teaching the skills digital creators need to design and develop their own ideas into real world physical prototypes. Students will engage in the design process from initial concept, "napkin" sketches, concept design drawings, 3D modeling, to full 3D printed prototype using professional software and fused filament and stereolythography (SLA) 3D printers.

Student work will consist of learning foundations principles in design processes, conceptual drawing skills, 3D print hardware and materials and the basic best practices for success in 3D printing taught through small design projects which will build skills and techniques ending with the student's creation of an original functional prototype.
